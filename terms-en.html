5) Prayer times and qibla

Prayer times and the qibla are calculated on your phone with established astronomical methods. Their accuracy depends on your location or chosen city, your chosen calculation method (methods differ by minutes), and your phone's compass, which is affected by nearby metal.

The qibla is computed from true north and corrected for your area's magnetic declination, but a compass is approximate: in a new place, a nearby mosque or a known mihrab is more reliable than any phone. Adhan alerts may be late or not arrive if your phone's system restricts the app to save battery.

We are not responsible for a prayer performed at a wrong time or in a wrong direction because of the device or its settings.
