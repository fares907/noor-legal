Privacy Policy — Noor

Effective: 4 October 2026

This policy describes what the Noor app does with data. It was written from the app's actual behaviour, not from a template. Noor is run by the Noor app team, reachable at noor0quran@gmail.com.

Summary

  1. The current version collects no data about you. No account, no server of ours that the app talks to, no analytics, no crash reports.
  2. No ads and no tracking. The app contains no advertising or tracking SDK and uses no advertising identifier.
  3. Your location never leaves your phone. Prayer times and the qibla are calculated on the device.

1) What stays on your phone only

Stored on your phone, never sent to us or anyone else:

  • Your settings: language, theme, calculation method, Asr school, adhan options, preferred reciter, font size, tajweed colouring.
  • Your approximate location or the city you chose, for prayer times and qibla.
  • Your place in the Mushaf, bookmarks and khatma plan.
  • Surahs you downloaded for offline listening, and their verse timings.

Backup: the app excludes its data from cloud backup (Google Drive). A direct transfer from your old phone to a new one carries over your settings and your place in the Mushaf.

Deleting it: uninstalling the app, or "Clear data" in your phone's settings, deletes all of the above permanently.

2) Connecting to the internet, and to whom

In the current version the app contacts one party only:

  • mp3quran.net — to stream and download recitations, and to fetch the verse timings that show which verse is being recited. These are content requests: their servers see your IP address and the request, as with any link you open. We send them nothing about you. Their own privacy policy applies to these requests.

The Mushaf, tafsir, prayer times, qibla, adhkar and tajweed colouring all work offline.

Accounts are not available in the current version. If they are added, they will be entirely optional, the app will stay fully usable without one, and this policy will be updated before they are offered. What would be kept then:

  • Synced data (place, bookmarks, khatma, settings) is encrypted on your phone with a key derived from your password before it is sent. The server stores ciphertext and has neither the key nor a way to derive it.
  • Sign in with Google (optional): Google gives us your email, name and public profile picture. We ask for no other access to your account.
  • Verification codes are sent through an email provider, which sees your address and the message.
  • Account deletion is available inside the app and deletes all of the above permanently.

5) What we never do

  • Sell your data or share it for marketing.
  • Use advertising identifiers, device fingerprinting or cross-app tracking.
  • Use Firebase Analytics, Crashlytics, Google Analytics or any analytics service.

6) Children

The app is suitable for all ages and collects data from no one, child or adult.

7) Security

  • Every connection the app makes uses HTTPS; unencrypted connections are disabled in its configuration.
  • Any secret it stores is kept in the device's secure keystore.
  • No system is perfectly secure, but this is what the app actually does.

8) Your rights

Your data is on your phone and in your hands: change it in the settings, delete it by uninstalling or clearing the app's data. For any privacy question, write to noor0quran@gmail.com; we reply within 30 days at most.

9) Changes

Any material change — in particular, offering accounts — will be announced in the app before it takes effect, and the effective date above updated.
